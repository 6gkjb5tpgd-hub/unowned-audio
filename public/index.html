Unowned Audio

Free audiobooks of anarchist, system-critical and hacker writing, read by volunteers. All audio is hosted on the Internet Archive. This site only links to it.

Why?

A lot of radical writing is free in text form, but very little of it is free in audio format. It is a lot more accessible for some people to listen to audiobooks. That's the need this project was born from.

Contribute

  1. Read the recording guide.
  2. Make an account. This requires NO info from you, as your account is tied to a unique token, not an email and a password and no phone numbers, etc.
  3. Add a text you want to read (with a link to the source material) and claim it. Make one entry per chapter. Check the rights yourself, following the rights section.
  4. Record it and upload it to the Internet Archive with the tag unowned-audio.
  5. Add the archive.org link to your contribution here.
  6. You can have two open claims at a time, not an entire book. This is to make sure that the recording projects stay manageable. You can always claim more later!

Values

The purpose of this project is to make books and texts more accessible, so of course we want to make it a space everyone can safely exist and collaborate in and use without any barriers. All creatures welcome.

Baseline

Antifascism is the baseline. If you discriminate against anyone for their skin colour, ethnicity, creed, gender, sexuality, physical or mental ability or health, age, education, body shape, way of speaking, financial situation or status, where they're from, or any other reason people get discriminated against, you aren't welcome here.

What we do not want here

  • Harassment of any kind, especially sexual harassment.
  • Sexually explicit or abusive content in contributions.
  • Eugenics, colonialism or imperialism. Including zionism. Palestinian people deserve to live like anyone else.
  • Apologism for genocide, discrimination, harassment or violence.
  • Authoritarianism, nationalism, idolising states, or glorifying capitalism.
  • Spam, including normal spam and AI slop. If a recording uses a synthetic voice, say so clearly, so that people can make an informed decision for what to listen to.

Reading a historical or critical text that discusses these things is fine. Endorsing them is not.

What we do want here

  • Respect people's boundaries and pronouns.
  • Put content notes on recordings that need them: violence, abuse, slurs in quotes, sudden loud sounds. If you're unsure, add one.
  • Make accessibility the default. Write descriptions in plain language.
  • Treat each other as equals. Nobody here is above or below anyone else.
  • If you see something, do something.

Hacker ethics

They apply where they can:

  • All information should be free.
  • Mistrust authority and promote decentralisation.
  • Judge people by their actions, not by degrees, age, race or position.
  • Make public data available and protect private data.
  • Don't litter other people's data.

Source: the CCC hacker ethic.

Emergency removal

The site admin can remove a contribution that is abusive, hateful or harmful. That is all. The admin does not check every upload, but promptly responds to abuse reports and to takedown requests from anyone, including legal rights holders. Please use admin@unowned-audio.org.

Rights and uploading

Nobody here reviews or approves your rights. You check them, you say so in public, and you are responsible for what you upload. This site hosts no audio. It only links to the Internet Archive. This isn't legal advice, and copyright differs between countries. If you're not sure, don't record it, or ask the author.

What you can record

  • Public domain. Check the author's death year (life plus 70 years in Germany, the UK and most of the EU). Check the translation too: a new translation has its own copyright even when the original is old.
  • Openly licensed. CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA and CC BY-NC are fine, since we are non-commercial. CC BY-ND and CC BY-NC-ND are not fine without asking, because an audio version can count as a derivative. Please link the page where the licence is stated.
  • With permission. The author or publisher has said yes in writing to a free audio recording under an open licence. Keep the message.

"It's free online" isn't permission. "The author probably doesn't mind" isn't permission.

Uploading to the Internet Archive

  1. Make a free archive.org account.
  2. Upload with the web uploader. Title: book title and chapter. Creator: the original author.
  3. Add the subject tag unowned-audio.
  4. Set the licence your recording is released under.
  5. Paste this into the description and fill it in:
Source text: [link]
Text licence: [name and link, or "public domain, author died YEAR"]
Evidence of permission: [link or "not needed"]
Declared by: [your handle]. I have checked the rights and I am responsible for this upload.
Read by: [handle]
Content notes: [or "none"]
Date: [date]

Keep your own raw recordings. We keep no copies of audio.

Objections

A rights holder who objects might prefer to go to the Internet Archive directly, as it's hosted on there, which has its own takedown process. If an item is removed, the link here stops working. You are also free to contact the admin at admin@unowned-audio.org. This website does not host the content, so all the admin can do is take down the link to it, and reported links are removed promptly.

Privacy

  • An account is a random key. No email, no password you choose, no real name.
  • This does not apply to archive.org, they have their own account stuff.
  • We store a scrambled version of your key, an optional display name you choose, and what you contribute. Nothing else.
  • We store no IP addresses and run no analytics or third-party scripts. The hosting provider sees your connection like any host does. Sadly we do not have the funds for private hosting.
  • We use one cookie, only for staying logged in. It lasts a week.
  • If you pick dark mode, your browser remembers that choice on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • There is no key recovery. If you lose it, the account is gone. You can delete your account and everything you added at any time.
  • Please use a display name that can't be linked to you elsewhere to protect your own privacy!
